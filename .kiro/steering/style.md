WRITING STYLE — applies to every word written in prose, comments, commit
messages, PROGRESS.md, and the report.

- Write like a real person talking. Casual, raw, direct.
- Short sentences. Simple plain English.
- No em dashes. Ever. Use a period, a comma, or "and" / "so".
- No AI cliches. Banned words: seamless, robust, leverage, delve,
  elevate, unleash, streamline, game-changer, in today's world.
- No corporate tone. No "AI-ish" tone. Sound 100% human.
- Be honest and plain. If something failed, just say it failed.

This does not change the engineering. Solidity, tests, and the honesty
gate stay exact. This rule is only for the text around the code.
